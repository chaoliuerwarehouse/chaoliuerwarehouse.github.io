Payment Instructions

We accept the following payment methods for your convenience. All transactions are secure.

1. MTN Mobile Money

Follow these steps to complete your payment via MTN Mobile Money:

  1. Dial *170# on your MTN line
  2. Select option for "Send Money"
  3. Choose "Mobile User"
  4. Enter our merchant number: 055 123 4567
  5. Enter the exact amount for your order
  6. Enter your Mobile Money PIN to authorize
  7. Save the Transaction ID shown on your screen
  8. Send the Transaction ID to us via WhatsApp or include it in your order notes

Note: Payments are processed within 1-2 hours after we receive your Transaction ID.

2. Vodafone Cash

To pay using Vodafone Cash:

  1. Dial *110# on your Vodafone line
  2. Select "Transfer Money"
  3. Choose "To Vodafone Cash Number"
  4. Enter our merchant number: 020 123 4567
  5. Enter the exact amount for your order
  6. Confirm the recipient details and amount
  7. Enter your Vodafone Cash PIN to complete
  8. You will receive a confirmation message with a reference number
  9. Send us this reference number via WhatsApp or include it in your order

3. Bank Transfer

For direct bank transfers, use the following account details:

Important: After making the transfer, please send us:

  • The exact amount transferred
  • Date and time of transfer
  • Your name or order number as reference
  • A screenshot or photo of the transfer confirmation

Payment Confirmation Process

To ensure your order is processed quickly after payment:

  1. Complete payment using any of the methods above
  2. Send payment proof (Transaction ID, reference number, or transfer details) to us via:
    • WhatsApp: +233 123 456 789
    • Email: payments@ghanastore.com
    • Online: Include in your order notes during checkout
  3. Include your order number or full name with the payment proof
  4. We will verify and confirm your payment within 1-2 business hours
  5. Once confirmed, we will process your order immediately

Payment Security Tips

  • Always verify our official contact numbers before sending money
  • Never share your Mobile Money or Vodafone Cash PIN with anyone
  • Keep your transaction IDs and reference numbers secure
  • Contact us immediately if you encounter any issues with your payment
  • We will never ask for your PIN via phone, WhatsApp, or email
