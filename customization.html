The Customization Process

  1. Consultation: Share your ideas, requirements, and budget
  2. Design Proposal: We create design options based on your input
  3. Material Selection: Choose from available fabrics, materials, and finishes
  4. Sample Approval: For bulk orders, we create a sample for your approval
  5. Production: We craft your custom items with attention to detail
  6. Delivery: Receive your finished custom products

Lead Time: Personal custom items typically take 7-14 days. Bulk orders require 3-4 weeks depending on complexity and quantity.
